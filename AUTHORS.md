# Authors and Contributors

This file lists the authors of the **Practical scheme for efficient distillation of GHZ states** project, their affiliations, and their principal contributions.

## Áron Rozgonyi

**Role:** Code Developer and Researcher  
**Affiliations:**

- Institute of Physics, [Eötvös Loránd University](https://www.elte.hu/en/), Budapest, Hungary
- Department for Quantum Optics and Quantum Information, [HUN-REN Wigner Research Centre for Physics](https://wigner.hu/), Budapest, Hungary
- [Portuguese Quantum Institute (PQI)](https://pqi.pt), Lisbon, Portugal
- Physics of Information and Quantum Technologies Group, Centro de Física e Engenharia de Materiais Avançados (CeFEMA), Lisbon, Portugal

**Email:** [aron.rozgonyi@pqi.pt](mailto:aron.rozgonyi@pqi.pt)  

**Contributions:**

- Development and implementation of the numerical GHZ-state distillation protocols
- Conceptualization of the nonlinear GHZ-state distillation protocol
- Implementation of the memory-efficient N-qubit simulation
- Numerical investigation of protocol convergence and scalability
- Implementation and analysis of coherent, incoherent, and randomized noise models
- Validation of the optimized protocol against numerical calculations
- Generation and analysis of numerical results and visualizations
- Repository organization and code documentation
- Preparation and revision of the associated manuscript
